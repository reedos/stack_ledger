01 A useful task

A person or business defines a goal: resolve a support case, develop software, analyze evidence or perform a physical task.

02 Model & workflow

A workflow or agent uses model outputs to choose or execute steps within its allowed scope.

03 Tools & systems

Software tools retrieve records or make changes. Physical applications additionally need sensors, controls and actuators.

04 Human review

Review and permissions depend on the stakes. Escalate uncertainty and consequential actions where appropriate.

05 Verified outcome

Tests, measurements and domain review establish whether work succeeded. A convincing answer alone is insufficient.

06 Value & feedback

Track quality, time, cost and real outcomes. Scientific and clinical claims need validation appropriate to their stage.
