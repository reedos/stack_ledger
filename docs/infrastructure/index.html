The factories of intelligence.

AI factories bring power, chips, storage, networking and cooling together. They provide the compute for models to produce useful intelligence, generated reasoning and actions.

INSIDE THE LAYER / HOW IT FITS TOGETHER

Inside an AI factory.

A conceptual campus cutaway: power and cooling sustain the racks, while storage and networks keep data moving. Software coordinates useful work.

Power / energyData / processHeat removalReview / feedback

Original conceptual illustration, not a site plan or engineering specification. Arrows show selected relationships, not measured flows or capacity. Numbered components are explained below; select one in the diagram to jump to it.

01 Power yard

Utility supply, transformers, switchgear and backup systems feed rack power distribution.

02 Cooling plant

Heat exchangers and cooling equipment remove heat. Air, liquid and heat-rejection designs vary by site.

03 External fiber

Campus and wide-area links connect users and other sites. Optical transport can link geographically separate facilities.

04 Storage systems

Persistent storage serves datasets, model files and checkpoints to compute.

05 Compute halls

Racks combine accelerators, CPUs and memory. Scale-up links join tightly coupled processors; scale-out networks connect servers.

06 Fabric & operations

Switch fabrics move data; scheduling, monitoring and access controls coordinate workloads and resources.

Data, accounting differences & sources

Selected hyperscalers, worldwide company spending. History uses reported cash PP&E; guidance uses each issuer’s stated definition. No combined total is calculated across different fiscal periods or accounting bases.

  • Amazon: Annual cash-flow statement PP&E purchases/additions; excludes noncash lease additions and finance-lease principal repayments. Amazon figures are gross of sales and incentives. Guidance: CY2026 cash capex guidance reported by AP from the July earnings call; includes businesses beyond AWS. Primary transcript remains a follow-up.
  • Alphabet: Annual cash-flow statement PP&E purchases/additions; excludes noncash lease additions and finance-lease principal repayments. Company-wide cash investment as reported. Guidance: CY2026 guidance reported by AP from July earnings. Primary transcript remains a follow-up.
  • Meta: Annual cash-flow statement PP&E purchases/additions; excludes noncash lease additions and finance-lease principal repayments. Meta historical cash PP&E is net of disposal proceeds where reported; retained on the later comparative basis. Guidance: CY2026 guidance includes finance-lease principal payments, unlike the historical cash PP&E series.
  • Microsoft: Annual cash-flow statement PP&E purchases/additions; excludes noncash lease additions and finance-lease principal repayments. Company-wide cash investment as reported. Guidance: CY2026 guidance after lease reclassification; finance leases included, operating leases excluded. Investment expectations unchanged apart from accounting impact.
  • Oracle: Annual cash-flow statement PP&E purchases/additions; excludes noncash lease additions and finance-lease principal repayments. Oracle uses reported GAAP capex, before financing/prepayment adjustments.
