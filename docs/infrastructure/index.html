The factories of intelligence.

AI factories bring power, chips, storage, networking and cooling together. They provide the compute for models to produce useful intelligence, generated reasoning and actions.

Original conceptual illustration, not a site plan or engineering specification. Arrows show selected relationships, not measured flows or capacity. Numbered components are explained below; select one in the diagram to jump to it.

01 Power yard

Utility supply, transformers, switchgear and backup systems feed rack power distribution.

02 Cooling plant

Heat exchangers and cooling equipment remove heat. Air, liquid and heat-rejection designs vary by site.

03 External fiber

Campus and wide-area links connect users and other sites. Optical transport can link geographically separate facilities.

04 Storage systems

Persistent storage serves datasets, model files and checkpoints to compute.

05 Compute halls

Racks combine accelerators, CPUs and memory. Scale-up links join tightly coupled processors; scale-out networks connect servers.

06 Fabric & operations

Switch fabrics move data; scheduling, monitoring and access controls coordinate workloads and resources.
