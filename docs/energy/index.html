01 Generation

Nuclear, wind, solar, hydro and other generators supply electricity. Their output profiles differ.

02 Transmission

High-voltage lines move power between supply and demand regions.

03 Substation

Transformers and switchgear adapt voltage and connect the local delivery system.

04 Storage & flexibility

Storage shifts energy in time; flexible loads can reduce demand when the system is constrained.

05 Distribution

Local circuits and protection equipment deliver power to customers.

06 Factories & communities

AI campuses, homes and industry need reliable service. Delivered MWh and connection dates matter.
